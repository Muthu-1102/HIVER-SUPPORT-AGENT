# Golden Evaluation Set Candidate-Generation Sampling Plan

## 1. Overview & Objectives

This document establishes the canonical specification, deterministic sampling strategy, and validation architecture for constructing the **Golden Evaluation Set Candidates** (`data/processed/golden_set_candidates.jsonl`) from the reconstructed SpotifyCares dialogue corpus (`data/processed/spotify_conversations.jsonl`).

The Golden Evaluation Set serves as the benchmark for downstream intent classification, routing policies, diagnostic scope tagging, escalation detection, and customer support performance evaluation.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Candidate Set Notice:** Records generated by this pipeline represent candidate selections equipped with rich stratum metadata and heuristic intent tags. **No final human gold labels are assigned automatically.** Human annotation and gold-standard verification follow in the subsequent evaluation phase.

---

## 2. Sampling Frame & Unit of Sampling

* **Source Corpus:** `data/processed/spotify_conversations.jsonl` (28,280 reconstructed conversations, 42.89 MB).
* **Sampling Frame:** The **full reconstructable SpotifyCares corpus**, comprising all 28,249 eligible conversation threads with verified customer and Spotify interactions (satisfying `contains_customer_and_spotify == True` and excluding documented taxonomy-design threads).
  * *Note:* The 400-thread discovery sample (`data/processed/intent_discovery_sample.jsonl`) was strictly a preliminary exploratory artifact and is **NOT** the Golden Set sampling frame.
* **Unit of Sampling:** One complete reconstructed conversation thread (`conversation_thread_id`, root tweet, ordered customer/brand messages, timestamps, and reconstruction flags).
* **Target Sample Size:** Exactly **200 unique conversation threads**.

---

## 3. Determinism & Ranking Protocol

To guarantee mathematical reproducibility across operating systems, Python runtime environments, and pipeline reruns without pseudorandom drift:

1. **Fixed Deterministic Salt:**
   $$\text{Fixed Salt} = \text{"hiver-golden-set-v1"}$$

2. **Deterministic Hash Formula:**
   $$\text{Sampling Hash} = \text{SHA256}(\text{conversation\_id} + \text{fixed\_salt})$$
   *Example:* `SHA256("spotify_root_855" + "hiver-golden-set-v1") = "1188f0b192df3a75b59bda5b8127dfed6b72fb0b455baf51beebddc3095fb3e2"`

3. **Ranking & Selection:**
   Candidates within each stratum pool are sorted in ascending lexicographical order of their 64-character hexadecimal SHA-256 hash. The lowest-ranked eligible candidates are deterministically selected to fulfill the stratum quota.

## 5. Deduplication & Multi-Strata Representation Policy

* **Unique Candidates:** Every thread in the output JSONL is strictly unique. No duplicate `conversation_id`s are permitted.
* **Secondary Strata Preservation:** When a thread is selected, all topical/feature criteria it legitimately satisfies across the heuristic strata (intents, flags, boundaries, escalation, compound, structural audit) are recorded in the `sampling_strata` list. For instance, a conversation chosen under `intent_baseline_04_billing_payment` that also matches double-charge escalation patterns and multi-intent criteria will contain:
  ```json
  "sampling_strata": [
    "intent_baseline_04_billing_payment",
    "escalation_sensitive",
    "compound_multi_intent"
  ]
  ```
* **Stratum 10 Control Sample:** The `unstratified_control` stratum represents the final 14 candidate threads drawn without topical pre-filtering. Only the 14 records selected during the Stratum 10 draw are tagged with `"unstratified_control"`.

---

## 6. Multi-Turn Representation Constraint

* **Floor Requirement:** At least **35.0% (>= 70 threads)** of the final 200 candidates must be multi-turn conversations (`customer_brand_interaction_turn_count >= 2`).
* **Enforcement Mechanism:** If the initial deterministic selection falls below 70 multi-turn threads, single-turn records in flexible strata are deterministically replaced by the lowest-hash multi-turn candidates matching the same stratum constraints.
* **Empirical Result:** The baseline deterministic selection naturally achieves **70 multi-turn threads (35.0%)**, satisfying the floor requirement without forced substitution.

---

## 7. Exclusion Policy & Design Integrity

To prevent data leakage and benchmark contamination:
* **Exclusion Source:** `data/processed/golden_set_exclusions.txt`.
* **Excluded Records:** All 28 conversation threads previously inspected or referenced during exploratory taxonomy formulation (e.g., `spotify_root_784880`, `spotify_root_904057`, `spotify_root_1151701`, etc.) are permanently excluded from candidate sampling.
* **Structural Audit Exception:** While known taxonomy examples are excluded from the candidate output, the structural audit stratum identifies fresh, previously unseen contaminated threads in the corpus to evaluate agent resilience against real-world data noise.

## 9. Thin-Intent Retrieval Limitations & Manual Confirmation Workflow

### Thin Intents Identified:
1. **`02_catalog_metadata_error` (8 candidates):**
   * *Limitation:* Metadata errors (wrong album art, misspelled title, out-of-sync lyrics) represent a small fraction (<0.5%) of public customer tweets. Lexical filters may capture user complaints about "wrong song" that are actually playback routing errors.
   * *Workflow:* Human annotators must confirm whether the issue pertains to metadata content correction versus streaming malfunction.
2. **`09_artist_rights_holder_mgmt` (8 candidates):**
   * *Limitation:* Artist profile claims and Spotify for Artists inquiries are relatively infrequent on public customer support channels compared to consumer inquiries. Heuristics matching "artist profile" may capture listener inquiries about artist discographies.
   * *Workflow:* Human review must verify that the author is an artist/rights-holder communicating regarding creator portal tools, verification, or rights management.

## 10. Reproducibility Procedure

To reproduce the Golden Set Candidate generation deterministically:

```powershell
# 1. Run generator script
python scripts/create_golden_set.py --input data/processed/spotify_conversations.jsonl --output-candidates data/processed/golden_set_candidates.jsonl --exclusions data/processed/golden_set_exclusions.txt --salt hiver-golden-set-v1 --target-total 200

# 2. Run automated test suite
python -m pytest -q tests/test_golden_set.py
```

Running the generation script repeatedly will produce byte-for-byte identical output files.
